Flipper IR Converter

Turn a Flipper Zero .ir remote into Pronto hex or Global Caché sendir strings for control system drivers. Everything runs in this page; the file never leaves your browser.

Open a file, drop one on the box, or paste its text. On the Flipper's SD card, saved remotes are in /infrared.

Where each format goes

Crestron
Pronto hex, one function at a time, into the IR driver editor to build the .ir driver.
Q-SYS
Global Caché strings sent over TCP port 4998 to an iTach or Flex, each ending in a carriage return. The Lua table is ready to paste into a script.
Extron
No text import. Play the saved button from the Flipper into the IR Learner instead.

Parsed buttons are rebuilt from the same timings the Flipper firmware transmits. NEC codes include the standard repeat frame; Sony codes repeat three times and Pioneer twice, as the protocols require.
